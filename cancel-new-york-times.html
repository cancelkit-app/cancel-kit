How to cancel The New York Times

Quick answer: Sign in at myaccount.nytimes.com and look for a cancel option. If your plan doesn't have one, use the chat on the Times help page to cancel.

Step by step

  1. Sign in at myaccount.nytimes.com.
  2. Open your subscription and look for Cancel.
  3. No cancel option? Go to the "Cancel your subscription" page in the Times help center and start the chat.
  4. Turn down the discount offers.
  5. Ask for the end date and a confirmation email, and save the chat.

Before you cancel

  • Games and Cooking are separate subscriptions. Cancel each one.
  • Paying through Apple? Cancel in your iPhone settings instead.
  • You usually keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
