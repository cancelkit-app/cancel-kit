How to cancel Adobe

Quick answer: Sign in at account.adobe.com, open Plans, choose Manage plan, then Cancel plan. A yearly plan paid monthly can carry an early cancellation fee, and the screen shows it before you confirm.

Step by step

  1. Sign in at account.adobe.com.
  2. Open Plans and choose Manage plan on the plan you want to end.
  3. Choose Cancel plan and pick a reason.
  4. Read the screen before you confirm. It shows any fee and the date your access ends. Turn down the discount offers if you want out.
  5. Confirm, and save the confirmation email.

Before you cancel

  • Adobe says eligible plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase get a full refund.
  • On a yearly plan, cancelling near the end of the year avoids the early cancellation fee.
  • Bought it through Apple or another app store? Cancel it there instead.
