How to cancel LA Fitness

Quick answer: LA Fitness usually needs a signed cancellation form, handed in at your club or sent by mail. Certified mail gives you proof it arrived.

Step by step

  1. Sign in to your LA Fitness account, find the cancellation form, and print it.
  2. Fill in your name, your contact details and the barcode number from your key tag, then sign it.
  3. Mail it by certified mail with a return receipt to the address printed on the form, or hand it in at your club and ask for a copy.
  4. Send it well before your next billing date.
  5. Keep the receipt, and check your bank on the next two billing dates.

Before you cancel

  • Unsigned forms may not be processed.
  • Use the address printed on the official form, not one copied from another website.
  • Keep a copy of everything you send.
