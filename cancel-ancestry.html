How to cancel Ancestry

Quick answer: Sign in, open Account Settings and cancel your membership there. Ancestry's terms say you can also cancel by contacting their support team.

Step by step

  1. Sign in at ancestry.com.
  2. Open Account Settings.
  3. Find your membership and choose to cancel. If you have more than one, cancel each one.
  4. Keep the confirmation number shown at the end.

Before you cancel

  • Your family trees and DNA results stay in your account after you cancel.
  • Check your renewal date first. Some plans renew every 6 months even when you pay monthly.
