How to cancel Audible

Quick answer: Use your credits first, then cancel on the Audible website (not the app) under Account details. Unused credits are lost when your membership ends.

Step by step

  1. Spend any credits you have left.
  2. On a computer, sign in at audible.com and open Account details.
  3. Choose Cancel membership and click through to the final confirmation page.
  4. Save the confirmation email.

Before you cancel

  • Books you bought stay in your library after you cancel.
  • Paying through Apple or Google? Cancel in that store.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel your membership.
