How to cancel Peloton

Quick answer: If Peloton bills you, cancel in your Peloton account under your membership. If you pay through Apple or Google, cancel in that store.

Step by step

  1. Check who bills you: Peloton, Apple or Google.
  2. Sign in at onepeloton.com, open your profile menu and find your membership.
  3. Choose Manage membership, then Cancel membership.
  4. Pick a reason, turn down the pause offer, and confirm.
  5. Save the confirmation email.

Before you cancel

  • Cancelling the membership does not end a loan for a Bike or Tread. Check that separately.
  • You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
